## Key Decisions & Learnings — 19 May 2026

### Recovery
- **Back-to-back reds demand a rest day.** Two consecutive 34% and below readings are a clear physiological signal — the body is not absorbing the training load. At minimum, the third day should have been active recovery or rest.
- **Magnesium + Sleep+Restore are non-negotiable evening supplements.** Two missed nights in a row correlates with two red recovery scores. The suppression is likely causal, not coincidental.
- **RHR at 64bpm is the warning metric.** When RHR climbs above 62–63 for multiple days, it confirms systemic fatigue, not just a bad sleep night.

### Training
- **Training on back-to-back reds compounds the debt.** Completing Pull Day on 34% is defensible once; doing it twice in a row starts to build a deeper recovery hole. The strain of 13.7 on a red day is too high.
- **Listen to the red signal.** WHOOP reds are the clearest data point available — the body is telling you to rest. Overriding it should be the exception, not the rule.

### Nutrition
- **Hitting 140g protein two days in a row at restaurants shows the system is working.** The overnight oats breakfast anchor (50g) makes the rest of the day achievable even with unpredictable meals.
