# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Jason, sole user. Opens the app daily as a morning ritual: checking recovery score, overnight sleep, planned workout, and nutrition targets. Also checks in during the day to log food and steps, and reviews training after sessions. Context is typically a phone screen in natural light or a laptop. The user is analytically minded and wants the raw data immediately visible, not buried behind summaries.

## Product Purpose

Apex is a personal optimization dashboard. It aggregates health and performance data (WHOOP recovery, nutrition, training, sleep, steps, weight, supplements) into a single coherent daily view. Success looks like: glance at the app, know exactly what your body is doing and what to do next. No friction. No marketing. No padding.

## Brand Personality

Clinical · Precise · Serious. A sports performance lab, not a wellness app. The aesthetic earns trust through density and accuracy, not encouragement. Data is the product; the interface is invisible infrastructure.

## Anti-references

- Corpo SaaS dashboards (Salesforce, HubSpot): dense sidebars, blue-on-white palettes, enterprise widget overload, visual noise masquerading as information.
- Mass-market health apps (MyFitnessPal, Strava): pastel blues, achievement badges, social feed energy, motivational copy that talks down to the user.
- Biohacker data dumps (Oura app, Levels early): twelve metrics per screen with no hierarchy, every chart given equal weight, no clear primary signal.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers are the interface.** Metric values are the primary design element. Typography and spacing exist to make them scannable in under two seconds.
2. **Signal over noise.** If a UI element does not directly surface a data point or enable a quick action, question whether it belongs on screen.
3. **Earned warmth.** The warm brown palette provides ambient comfort without softening the precision. No decorative illustrations, no encouraging filler copy, no confetti.
4. **Strict hierarchy.** Every element on a given screen has a clear rank. Primary metric, supporting context, secondary data. Nothing competes for attention.
5. **Ritual-shaped.** Optimized for a focused daily check-in, not casual exploration. The most important data surfaces immediately at the top; deep-dive routes are secondary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Priority: legible metric numbers at all sizes, sufficient contrast on all text including small labels and muted values. Reduced motion honored via prefers-reduced-motion.
